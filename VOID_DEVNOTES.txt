=======================================
  VOID — DEVELOPMENT NOTES (browser rebuild)
  Phase 3 — November 1, 2026
=======================================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
Void was rebuilt from scratch as a 3D browser game (three.js). The Unity
project is parked, not deleted. Claude now writes, runs and playtests the
game itself; Todd only plays and gives notes. No Unity, no C#, no APK.

HOW TO PLAY IT
--------------
- Quick play: the claude.ai artifact link (opens in the browser).
- Full-screen app: host the files in this package (index.html,
  manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png) on any web host
  (e.g. free GitHub Pages), open on the S20 in Chrome, then
  menu > "Add to Home screen". It launches full screen, portrait, offline.
- "Tap to play" also asks the browser for full screen.

DESIGN RULES (unchanged)
------------------------
Zen: no timer, no failure, no score pressure. Camera ~48° above the ground.

PHASE 1 FEATURES
----------------
- Everything is built from 1x1x1 cubes (~14,400 on the farm map).
- Each cube falls in on its own when its center is over the hole, so big
  things crumble in piece by piece (fixes the "house should fall in" bug).
- Pieces no longer connected to the ground drop as a chunk (tree crowns,
  roofs, the tethered balloon when you eat its rope anchor).
- Debris that lands outside the hole rests on the ground and drifts in
  when the hole gets close.
- Camera keeps the hole at ~25% of screen width at every size, so the
  view pulls back as you grow (fixes the late-game "hole fills the screen").
- 9 sizes. Radii: 1.6, 2.1, 2.7, 3.4, 4.2, 5.2, 6.4, 7.8, 9.4 cubes.
  Size-ups at 1.2/3.5/7/13/21/32/46/64% of the map eaten.
- Thin progress ring (about 2-3 px) just outside the hole; flashes on size-up.
- "Size N" label under the hole.
- Top-left: the three colors with the most cubes left, plus % cleared.
- When only a few cubes remain, an orange arrow points to the nearest one.
- Drag is relative: the hole never jumps to your finger.
- Soft sounds (mute button), light vibration on size-up.
- Auto quality: shadows switch off if the phone drops below ~38 fps.

FARM LAYOUT (camera looks north)
--------------------------------
Start meadow: chickens, flowers, coop, well, vegetable garden, mailbox,
wheelbarrow. West: pumpkin patch, corn field with scarecrow, pig pen with
mud. Center: red gambrel barn, silo, tractor, hay bales, trough, sunflower
row. East: duck pond, apple orchard, beehives, tethered hot-air balloon.
North: farmhouse, windmill, cow and sheep paddock. Border trees.

PLAYTEST (automated, by Claude)

Size 9 and cleared all cubes; win screen appears. No stuck debris.
Not yet measured: real frame rate on the S20.

FILES
-----
src/: builder.js (shared voxel tools + big-block merging), palette.js,

one index.html plus manifest, sw.js and icons in dist/.

PHASE 1.1 CHANGES (after Todd's first laptop play)
--------------------------------------------------
- Controls are now a joystick: press anywhere and pull; the hole keeps
  moving that way while held, faster the farther you pull. A faint ring
  and dot show the stick. No more "invisible wall" at the window edge
  (old drag only moved as far as the mouse could travel in the window).
- Zoom is set by the screen's shorter side, so laptop and phone match.
- View width (short side) = 16 cubes + hole diameter / 0.22: much more of
  the farm visible at Size 1, and a clear step out at every size-up.
- Hole starts at (0, 40), a bit further from the board's near edge.

PHASE 2 CHANGES
---------------
Big blocks
- Maps now mix 1x, 2x and 4x cubes. Big blocks are merged from solid,
  mostly one-colour areas the map marks as "chunky".
- 2x blocks can be swallowed from Size 2, 4x blocks from Size 4.
- Until then a big block wobbles at the rim and a hint says which size
  you need. A toast announces each unlock; the line under the counters
  shows the next unlock ("2x blocks at Size 2").
- Counters and % cleared count unit cubes, so a 4x block is worth 64.
- Size-up targets never ask for more than 70% of what you can already eat.
- Farm: barn walls are 2 thick and the barn is packed with hay (4x
  blocks); the silo is full of grain; hay bales, cows, pigs, sheep,
  pumpkins, apple trees and the tractor have big blocks.

Second map: Reef
- Sandy floor with moving light (caustics), rising bubbles, blue water fog.
- Start shallows: starfish, clams with pearls, urchins, sea grass, fish.
- Coral garden: brain corals, branching corals, table corals, sea fans,
  anemones with clownfish.
- West: kelp forest and a sea turtle. North: shipwreck (solid hull, 4x
  blocks), treasure chest and spilled gold, anchor, and a whale floating
  above the wreck. East: rocky reef wall, yellow submarine, jellyfish.
- Floating things hang in the water until the hole passes under them or
  the piece holding them up is eaten, then they drop.

Start and win screens
- Start screen is a map picker (Farm, Reef). Cleared maps show a check.
- Win screen shows cubes eaten and time, with "Try the <other map>" and
  "Play again". The game remembers the last map played.

Feel
- Big blocks land with a low thump and a small camera shake (4x).
- The rim pulses ("gulps") as you eat. Win plays a short fanfare.
- Reef pops are higher pitched than the farm's.

Phone app
- sw.js is network-first, so a new version shows up the next time the
  app opens online; it still works offline.
- Hosting: GitHub Pages from Todd's public "void" repository (set up
  Oct 31). Repo root holds the built game; src/ holds the source.

PLAYTEST (automated, Phase 2)
- Bot cleared Farm (17,333 cubes) and Reef (11,137 cubes) at phone size
  with no errors; reached Size 9 on both.

PHASE 2.1 FIXES (Todd's phone play, Oct 31)
-------------------------------------------
- Hole always stays fully on the board, so its tube no longer pokes out
  past the edge and the edge no longer draws across the hole.
- Joystick top speed is about 45% slower (Todd found it too fast).
- Swallowed cubes now ride along with the hole while they fall, so a
  fast-moving hole no longer leaves them behind on the ground.
- When 80 or fewer pieces are left, a bobbing orange marker floats over
  each one (plus the edge arrow), so the last cubes are easy to find.
- Reef: the low-contrast sand-coloured cubes became bright little shells.

PHASE 3 (Nov 1)
---------------
Zoom and ring
- Much less zoom-out per size: view (short side) = 24 + hole diameter x 2.2
  cubes (was 16 + diameter x 4.5). At Size 9 cubes look ~55% bigger than
  before and the hole fills about a third of the screen width.
- The progress ring had gone thin enough to vanish at small sizes (it
  scaled with the hole). It is now drawn at a fixed 3 screen pixels, warm
  gold (#ffc83d) over a faint white track, with a soft dark outline, a few
  pixels outside the rim, and always drawn on top.
- Board sides are now 40 deep (were 6), so the hole's tube never shows
  below the board edge.

Save and pause
- Each map saves itself in the browser every 5 s, when the app is hidden
  or closed, and when you pause. Saves record eaten pieces, loose debris,
  size, hole position and play time.
- Map picker shows "Resume · N% cleared" with a "Start over" link.
  Winning a map clears its save. A save is dropped if a later update
  changes that map's layout.
- Pause button (top right). Pause panel: "Keep playing" or "Back to maps".
- The "Press and pull" hint now only shows the first time ever.
- HUD squeezes slightly on narrow phones; 5-digit counts use a smaller font.

Third map: Valley
- Flat meadow (glen) at the front with wildflowers, lupine patches,
  mushrooms, a mossy log, stumps, boulders and berry bushes.
- Ring of pines, birches, round trees and a few golden autumn trees.
- Light brown terraced hills (2-cube steps) with grassy tops and trees.
- Mountain range at the back: grey stone, green lower slopes, snow above
  height 25-30. Hardy pines on the lower slopes.
- A waterfall at the mountain foot feeds a stream that cuts a channel
  through the hills, passes under a plank bridge and fills a lake with
  lily pads and reeds.
- About 99,000 unit cubes (23,000 pieces; hidden insides are big blocks).
  Early sizes come sooner on this map (its own size-up targets).
- Bot cleared it at phone size with no errors.
